Flight counts include retained uploaded and imported records, including zero-duration observations. They do not establish installs, signups, mission counts, or a historical confirmation audit. Duration is the recorded observation interval. Missing uploads and deleted records limit coverage; late uploads and corrections can revise earlier periods.

“New” means the first flight in retained history. “Retained” means activity in both adjacent periods; retention is retained organizations divided by previously active organizations. “Quiet” does not mean a lost customer. Weeks begin Monday; months use calendar boundaries and may differ in length. Unassigned flights are excluded from organization adoption totals.

Pilot counts use matched historical member identities only. Unresolved and legacy labels are not counted as distinct pilots. Exported pilot numbers are pseudonymous, scoped to the organization, and stable while the server signing key remains unchanged. Organization names remain visible. Treat exports as internal data.
