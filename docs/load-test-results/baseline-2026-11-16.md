## What the numbers say

- Quiet fleets of 10, 20, and 40 organizations (20, 40, and 80 tablets) all parked after 30 s with no admission rejects. Hello-ack p95 stayed under 400 ms.
- At 80 and 160 organizations the concurrency cap of 80 admitted a full instance. Both runs parked 80 tablets. The rest queued and were counted as 429s (320 at 80 orgs, 1120 at 160 orgs). Hello-ack p95 for the tablets that got in stayed near 330 ms. RSS stayed near 167 MiB.
- Scenario B with 3 hot orgs of 6 tablets plus 7 quiet orgs: 32 hello acks, heartbeat-ack p50 27 ms, three flight uploads p50 160 ms (weather stubbed), and all 14 quiet tablets parked. NOTAM calls returned 503 because this process has no FAA credentials, then the clients retried on the 15 s error path.
- Warm restart of that B population: lockstep reconnect reached hello_ack with p50 752 ms after the new process was ready. The jittered 0–5 s variant p50 was 2116 ms. Both reconnected the tablets that were still up. Jitter spreads the herd; it does not remove it.
- Android ack-timeout (hello_ack delayed 12 s): 0 successful acks in the window, 18 ack timeouts and 66 connect attempts across 18 tablets. Android's 0-delay retry is what produces the extra attempts.

CPU was pinned to one core. A 1 GiB memory cgroup was not available; measured RSS stayed under 170 MiB. Event-loop lag and lock wait were not recorded because instrumentation stayed off.
